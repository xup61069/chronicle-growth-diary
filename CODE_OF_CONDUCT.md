# Chronicle Community Code of Conduct

Chronicle exists to help people preserve personal memories. Contributions, discussions, issues, and reviews must therefore treat both collaborators and the sensitive nature of personal archives with care.

## Expected conduct

Participants are expected to communicate constructively, welcome good-faith questions, respect differing life experiences, and give feedback about code or ideas rather than people. Screenshots, logs, fixture data, and reproduction steps must be scrubbed of private diary content, credentials, secrets, and identifiable information unless the owner has expressly agreed to share them.

| Please do | Please do not |
|---|---|
| Explain technical decisions and privacy trade-offs clearly. | Publish another person’s memories, images, account data, tokens, or passwords. |
| Report security concerns privately and provide the minimum evidence needed to reproduce them. | Use harassment, discrimination, threats, or personal attacks in any project space. |
| Ask before reusing personal examples or screenshots in documentation. | Represent generated or fabricated material as a real person’s lived experience. |

## Reporting and enforcement

Report conduct or security concerns privately to the repository owner through the contact channel listed on the project’s GitHub profile. Reports should include a concise description, relevant links or timestamps, and any immediate safety concern. Maintainers may request clarification, remove content, limit participation, or take other proportionate action to protect the community and user data.

## Scope

This code applies to all project spaces, including issues, pull requests, discussions, releases, and project-related events.
